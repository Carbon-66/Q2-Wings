Wings for the Shanling Q2 (Q2 Pod card game)

Slide down the hills, fly off the tops and hop from island to island before the sun sets.

Install: copy this .wings folder to the root of the card, then open Library > Games > Wings.

Controls
  Touch and hold anywhere, or hold Centre   dive: Pip gets heavy and speeds down slopes
  Let go                                    fly: Pip launches off the next rise
  Pause button (top right), Play/Pause      pause
  Hold Return                               leave the game

How to play
  Hold on the way down a hill, let go before the next one rises. Landing smoothly on a
  downslope and then flying off the next rise is a perfect slide; three in a row starts a
  fever (double points). Coins, long flights and each new island score too.
  The sun is your clock: it sinks as the day runs out, and every island you reach gives some
  daylight back. When night falls Pip gets sleepy, and the run ends when Pip stops.

best.txt keeps your best score; wings.log records each run and the frame rate.
